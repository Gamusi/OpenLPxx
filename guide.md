# OpenLP Anglican – Church Presentation Software

**A simple, offline presentation system designed to make church services easier to manage.**

OpenLP Anglican is a customized version of the free and open-source OpenLP software, prepared especially for Anglican churches.

It helps churches display **hymns, Bible verses, prayers, liturgy, announcements, pictures and videos** on a projector or large screen during worship services.

The software comes with useful Anglican worship materials already included, making it easier for churches to prepare and conduct services.

### What can it do?

- **Display hymns:** Comes with 412 Anglican hymns. Search for a hymn and display its words for the congregation to sing along.
- **Read and project Bible verses:** Includes the World English Bible, allowing users to search for Scripture passages and display them during services.
- **Display prayers and liturgy:** Includes 39 Anglican liturgical items, such as prayers and creeds, from *Come and Worship*.
- **Prepare church services:** Comes with ready-made Morning Prayer and Holy Communion service templates. Churches can also create and save their own orders of service.
- **Show announcements and media:** Display church notices, pictures, presentations and videos.
- **Preview before displaying:** The operator can prepare the next hymn, prayer or Bible reading without interrupting what the congregation is currently seeing.
- **Add your own content:** Churches can add hymns, edit prayers, prepare presentations and save their own materials.

### Works without internet

OpenLP Anglican is designed for churches where internet access may be expensive, unreliable or unavailable.

Once installed, the software works offline. You only need a Windows computer and a projector or suitable display.

### Easy to install and use

The software comes as a Windows installer. It includes the necessary programs and prepared worship materials, so churches do not need to install Python or other technical software separately.

Each church can manage its own hymns, prayers and service arrangements. These changes are designed to remain safe when the software is updated or reinstalled.

### Who is it for?

OpenLP Anglican is suitable for Anglican churches of different sizes, particularly those looking for an affordable and practical way to improve congregational participation during worship.

It can be operated by a church secretary, youth member, media team member or anyone with basic computer skills.

### Our goal

**To make church presentations simple, affordable and accessible, so that congregations can follow hymns, Scripture readings and prayers together without unnecessary technical difficulties.**
