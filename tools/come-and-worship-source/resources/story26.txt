
PRAYER OF HUMBLE ACCESS
[All kneel]
All: We do not come to your table, merciful Lord,
trusting in our own goodness,
but in your mercy.
We are not good enough
even to eat the crumbs
that fall from your table.
But you never change;
your nature is always to have mercy.
25
We, therefore, humbly ask You,
gracious Lord,
to let us eat the ﬂesh of your
dear Son, Jesus Christ,
and drink His blood.
So that our sinful bodies
may be made clean by His body,
and our souls washed
by His precious blood,
and that we may, forever, dwell in Him
and He in us. Amen.
