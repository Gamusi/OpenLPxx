
ORDER OF SERVICE FOR
BAPTISM FOR ALL AGES
DURING MORNING OR
EVENING PRAYER
[This Order of Service begins as in Morning and Evening;
Prayer to the end of the second reading. However, proper
Psalms, readings, sentence and collect may be used instead of
those of the day| .
2nd LESSON
Reader: This is the word of the Lord.
All: Thanks be to God.
ANNOUNCEMENTS (AND NOTICES)
Hymn
[AII stand]
Priest: Christ has died:
Christ is risen:
Christ will come again.
All : Alleluia! Christ is alive.
Priest: The Lord is loving to everyone:
31
All: and his mercy is all over his works.
Priest: Dear friends, all people are born with a
sinful nature; and our Saviour Christ has said
that no one can enter the Kingdom of God
unless they are bom again through water and
the Holy Spirit. So let us pray to God the
Father, through our Lord Jesus Christ, that by
his great mercy he will give to these children
[and persons] what they cannot have by birth;
that they may be baptised with water and
the Holy Spirit, and enter and become active
members of Christ’s holy Church.
