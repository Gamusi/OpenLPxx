
APPENDIX II
THE PROPER PREFACES FOR USE .
AT HOLY COMMUNION
Christmas
Who was born as at this time to the Virgin Mary through the power
of
the Holy Spirit taking on human nature and sharing in our
weakness but without sin by his blood. We are washed from the
guilt of our sins.
Easter
Praising you especially for his death by which we have the hope of
etemal life.
Ascension
Who after his resurrection clearly appeared to all his apostles and
in their sight ascended into heaven. to prepare a place for those he
died to save. so that where he is we also might ascend and share
his glorious reign.
Pentecost (Whit Sunday)
According to whose true promise, the Holy Spirit came down at
this time from heaven with sound of a rushing wind and appearance
of tongues of ﬁre. He rested on the apostles to lead them
into all truth and to give them zeal and boldness to preach to every
people the good news of salvation by which we have been brought
out of the darkness into the true knowledge of you and of your Son
Jesus Christ.
47
Trinity Sunday
For you and your Son and your Spirit-are three persons and one
God. What we believe about the glory of the Father, we also
believe about the glory of the Spirit; and what we believe about
the glory of the Spirit, we also believe without diiference or in-
equality, about the glory of our Saviour, Jesus Christ, the Son.
