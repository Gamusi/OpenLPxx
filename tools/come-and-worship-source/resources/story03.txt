
COMMENTARY ON THE HOLY COMMUNION
ARRIVING IN CHURCH
When you get to Church, Kneel and pray:
`In the name of the Father and of the Son and of the Holy Spir-it. Amen. Lord, I am here in your house. Help me to pray. Lord, I am here with your family. Help us all to love you more.' You can now find the readings for the day.
If there is time, you can pray for the people around you. For the person on your left, the one on your right, the one in front, the one behind. Just say: 'God bless the person in front of me.' It doesn't matter if you don't know them. God does. We are all his family.
As the priest enters say: 'God bless our priest.' Many other Churches haven't got a priest. You have today. Thank God for him.

THE PREPARATION
Make all your responses in a bold voice. Think what you say.
You can't hide from God. He knows you as you really are. That's why you say: Lord have mercy.
Now you are ready to join the angels' song. It rings round the world.
There is a different collect for each Sunday and Festival of the Church's year. This collects together the main theme of the Sun-day and the following week. If you have the words read silently with the priest.
THE MINISTRY OF THE WORD
In all Christian worship the Bible has a central place.
Follow the reading in your Bible. It helps you to concentrate.
Note that there may be a silence after each reading. Keeping silent can be valuable in our busy lives.
Everybody helps each other in this corporate activity of silence.
Do your part. Think over what you have just heard.
The sermon involves three sorts of people. God, the preacher, and you. Do your part. Your part is to listen, meditate and act.
Since AD 325 Christians have said the Nicene Creed (after a Council at Nicaea) what they believe about God, Christ, his coming, his birth, his death, his resurrection and his coming again. Say it boldly. It's your hymn of faith. Apply its statements to your own thinking.

THE PRAYERS INTERCESSIONS
Christianity is a religion of involvement, (not escape) So our prayers need to be down to earth. In silence make the prayers your own by adding any special names or details you know ab4t • the subject mentioned.
Too many people think religion is a succession of 'don'ts.' Christ's religion is just the opposite. So when the Ten Commandments are said, note carefully the positive part, the' dos' which follow from the 'don'ts: '
CONFESSION AND ABSOLUTION
God loves each one of us. He expects the best from us. All of us have failed him. All of us tell him so frankly. Make this prayer include those failures which you know about in your life. Remember, you can't hide from God.
Then
God's minister - the priest - pronounces God's forgiveness. He speaks in God's name to you: `Strengthen you in all goodness' You can start again in God's strength.
PRAYER OF HUMBLE ACCESS
If you meet Christ face to face and he asked you to have a meal with him wouldn't this prayer express your feelings exactly? We all fee the same. So we all say this together.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
THE PEACE
We are all in the Christian Church together. Christ's peace helps us to live at peace with our neighbours. The Celebrant/priest starts the sharing.
And
We share it now with him and each other. Don't be afraid to turn around or move out of your seat to greet other worshippers.
THE TAKING OF THE BREAD AND WINE
Bread and wine and offerings are brought - usually by parishioners - to the Holy table. It is time for your work, your recreation, your earnings, your life to be offered to God.
THE THANKSGIVING
All is ready. Christ invites us to his table. We have accepted. We are not worthy. We know that. So we have confessed our failures. He has forgiven what is past and given us strength to persevere in the future. We are going to join together in the great act which he himself started. So when the President says:
`The Lord is here'
We are ready to say, with thankful hearts:
`His Spirit is with us.'
xvi
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Then, as the President begins the great prayer, we are ready to join with the whole company of heaven in the great hymn of praise:
`Holy, Holy, Holy.'
Now the President is going to
do what Christ did;
say what Christ said.
Our only fitting utterance is:
`Christ has died:
Christ is risen
Christ will come again.'
With the Celebrant we celebrate Christ's perfect sacrifice proclaim Christ's resurrection look towards Christ's coming again; we ask him to renew our lives that we may live and work for him in the world. Finally we all join in a cry of adoration and praise. Then Silence.
THE BREAKING OF THE BREAD
Christ's body was crucified for us. Our lives must be sacrificed as his was. We do this by becoming carriers of his risen power. Christ takes, Christ blesses, Christ breaks and gives back to us what we offered him - ourselves, restored and recreated by his life.
xvii
------------------------------------------------------------------------
THE GIVING OF THE BREAD AND THE CUP
Words are inadequate to describe moments like this So as God comes down as he promised, to meet man, as God comes to you and your fellow Christians, we simply say the prayer Christ himself taught us:
`Our Father....'
`Draw near with faith' says the Celebrant speaking Christ's invitation to you. Faith is trust in a person. For the Christian that person is God.
While the Celebrant (and any helping him) are receiving the bread and wine, kneel or sit while you wait in your place. Be very still.
This is the time to speak to God in your own words. Tell him you love him. Tell him you are determined to serve him better. Tell him you are grateful for all he has given you especially for his unfailing love.
Say: `You who do' give your very self, Lord Jesus, For food to those that seek the things above: What less than self-surrender dare I offer you awful love?'
xviii
------------------------------------------------------------------------
If there is more time before or after you have received your communion read some of the hymns in your book under the heading of Holy Communion.
`The Body of Christ keep you in eternal life.' `The Blood of Christ keep you in eternal life. '
Eternal life - life outside time and space. We small, unimportant creatures are made one with our God, the Creator who thinks us worth dying for that we might live for him.
As you receive bread and wine say:
`Amen';
You are receiving Christ into your life.
AFTER COMMUNION
You have shared Christ's life Now you pledge yourself to share his work in his world; to think, work and pray with him in your home, your job, your country. You need Christ's peace.
He gives it in the words spoken by the Celebrant who adds:
`Go in peace and serve the Lord'
Christ has given you himself
He relies on you.
You must not fail.
