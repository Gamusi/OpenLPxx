
THE WELCOME
[The priest and the congregation, representing the
whole Church, welcome the newly baptized]
Priest: God has received you by baptism into his Church.
All: We welcome you into the Lord’s Family.
We are members together of the body of Christ;
we are children of the same heavenly Father;
we are inheritors together of the Kingdom
of God.
We welcome you.
