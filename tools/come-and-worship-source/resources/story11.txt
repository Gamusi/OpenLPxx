
PRAYER RESPONSES
[All kneel]
Leader: :: Show us your constant love, O God;
People: and grant us your salvation.
Leader: :: O Lord, save our President;
People: and teach his counsellors wisdom-
Leader: :: Let your ministers be clothed with righteousness;
People: and let your servants shout for joy.
Leader: :: O Lord, make your ways known upon the earth;
People: Let all nations acknowledge Your saving power.
Leader: :: Give your people the blessing of peace;
People: and let your glory be over all the world.
Leader: :: Create pure hearts in us, 0 God; .
People: and do not take your Holy Spirit from us

Leader: :: THE COLLECT OF THE DAY
