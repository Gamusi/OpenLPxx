
OFFERTORY
Minister: Let us joyfully bring our gifts to God;
People: for God loves a cheerful giver.
Hymn
[All stand]
[During the hymn the minister places the bread and
wine on the altar and receives the gifts]
Minister: [laying the gifts on the altar]
To you, O God, belong greatness
power, glory and majesty,
for everything in heaven and on
earth are yours.
O Lord, accept our gifts
to represent the offering of our lives,
through Jesus Christ our Lord. Amen.
