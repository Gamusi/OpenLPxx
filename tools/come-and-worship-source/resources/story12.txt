
MORNING PRAYER
Almighty God,
we thank you for the gift of
a new day.
Defend us, we pray, against all harm,
direct our thoughts, speech and actions.
Help us to serve you faithfully
that in our work and worship
we may always please you,
in the name of Jesus Christ our Lord. Amen

OR

EVENING PRAYER

All: In your hands, O Lord,
we commit ourselves tonight.
Be our light in the hours of dark.
And protect us against all, dangers,
through Jesus Christ our Lord. Amen.

All: Eternal God,
you are the source of all holy
thoughts and desires,
of all true wisdom, and all good deeds;
grant us, your servants, true peace
which the world cannot give.
And by your grace, may we do
what you command us to do,
and under your protection live
in peace and quietness,
through Jesus Christ our Lord. Amen.
