Leader: :: Let us pray,

All: Be with us, Lord,
as we go out into the world;
May the lips that have sung your
praises always speak the truth;
May the ears which have heard
your word listen only to what is good;
and may our lives as well as our
worshlp be always pleasing in
your sight,
for the glory of Jesus Christ. Amen.
