AMAZE-OMULIMU GWE
AMAZE-omulimu gwe 
Alwanye-olutalo lwe;
Avudde mu mayengo,
Yeesiimye-okuwunguka.
Kitaffe tuwaayo gy'oli,
Omwana w(o) eyeebase 

Eyo teri kukaaba;
Awonye-ennaku-ez'ensi;
Atuuse mu maaso ge,
Oyo eyatonda-ensi.
Kitaffe tuwaayo gy'oli,
Omwana w(o) eyeebase 

Eyo-amaanyi g'okufa 
Tegakyamutuutako;
Omununuzi gy'ali
Eyawangula-okufa
Kitaffe tuwaayo gy'oli,
Omwana w(o) eyeebase 
 
Naffe-abasigaddewo
Tulinda nnyo n'essuubi;
Okumulaba Yesu,
Ku lunaku-olwentiisa.
Kitaffe tuwaayo gy'oli,
Omwana w(o) eyeebase
