KABAKA MUKAMA WAFFE
KABAKA Mukama waffe 
Atuyita ku mbaga ye;
Ategese ebya ssava,
Twanguwe okugendayo.

Kristo-okuyitako kwaffe 
Omwana gw'endiga Yesu,
Eyaweebwayo ku lwaffe,
Tulyoke ffe tuwangule.
 
Kristo yava mu ntaana ye 
N'obuwanguzi obungi 
N'amaayi,okulokola
Ababi abamwesiga 

Aleruuya, Aleruuya;
Osaanidde ekitiibwa,
Naawe Katonda Kitaffe,
N'Omwoyo Omutukuvu
