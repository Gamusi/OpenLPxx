MU NSI Y'OMU GGULU
MU nsi y'omu ggulu 
Tewabaawo kiro,
Emirimu tegikoya
Bakoza-okwagala.

Eri teri nnaku,
Essanyu lingi nnyo:
Katonda waffe-asangula 
Amaziga gonna.

Eri teri kibi:
Abaliyo bonna 
Bambadde-engoye-entukuvu,
Bayimbira Yesu.

Eri teri kufa:
Abaayingira-eyo,
Baamala bo okusikira
Obulamu-obutaggwaawo.
 
Yesu,musaale ggwe,
Otukulembere;
-Otuyise mu kabi konna 
Otutuuse mu ggulu
