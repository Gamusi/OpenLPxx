OBUDDE BUZIBYE
OBUDDE buzibye
Enjuba-egenze
Enzikiza-ekwata
Mu bbanga ly'ensi.
 
Buli nsolo yonna,
Era n'ennyonyi,
Byonna biwummula
Byebaka-otulo.

Ayi Mukama waffe,
Ontunuulire,
Kaakano nkusaba,
Ompe-ekisa kyo.
 
Ebyonoono byange,
Binkwasa-ensonyi;
Naye-olw'ekisa kyo,
Tongoba w'oli.
 
Bwe neebaka-otulo,
Mu kiro kino;
Bamalayika bo
Bajje bankuume.

Ate bwe bunaakya,
Nga ngolokose,
Mbeere mu maaso go
Omulongoofu.

Nsabira-abalwadde,
Ne bannamwandu;
Obawe-omutima
Ogukwesiga.
 
Oweebwe-ekitiibwa
Gwe-eyatonda-ensi;
Ogulumizibwe,
Gwe-eyazuukira.
