ENDAGAANO-ENTUKUVU
ENDAGAANO-entukuvu,
Gye njagalira ddala,
Byonna bye neetaaga nze,
Birabikira mu yo.

Omwoyo-Omutukuvu,
Yagiwandiisa edda;
Teyagunjibwa bantu,
Kyenaavanga ngyesiga.

Bwe nkyama enkomyawo,
Bwemba ngwa ennyimusa,
Bwe siraba mu kkubo,
Eba gye ndi-omusana.

Mu nnaku ensanyusa 
Yesu ng'anjigiriza,
Era-ewoomesa gye ndi 
Obulamu,n'okufa.
