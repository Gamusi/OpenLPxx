MUMUSSEEKO-ENGULE
MUMUSSEEKO-engule 
Omwana gw'endiga;
Muwulire-amatendo ge 
Agatenkanika.
Yimba mwoyo gwange,
N'amaanyi go gonna,
Oyatule Kabaka wo 
Eyakufiirira.

Mumusseeko-engule
Omwana w'omuntu,
Yawangula-abalabe be 
Abaamujeemera.
Banabbi ab'edda 
Bye baamulangako,
Byonna yabituukiriza 
Bwe yabeera mu nsi.
 
Mumusseeko-engule 
Nnannyini kwagala,
Enkovu z'ebiwundu bye 
Zonna zirabika.
Kino kya kitalo 
Ekitenkanika,
-Emikono gye-emifumite 
Gye gituwa-obulamu-

Mumusseeko-engule 
Mukama w'eggulu,
Eyatuwanguza fenna 
Abamweyabiza 
Mununuzi waffe,
Kkiriza-ettendo lyo 
-Emirembe-agitalabika 
Lye giri kussaako
