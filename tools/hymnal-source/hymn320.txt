KINO KYE NSIIMA-ENNYO
KINO kye nsiima-ennyo 
Bwe nkirowoozaako,
-Okusembera-okumpi 
N'okufa nga sitya.

Buli kiro mmala 
Ebbanga mu kkubo;
Eririntuusa-eyo 
Ewaffe mu ggulu.
 
Siriiko kye ntya nze 
Mu lugendo luno,
Gye mgenda mu ggulu 
Kitange gy'abeera.

Byonna ndibiraba 
Bye nasomangako,
-Abatukuvu bonna 
Abankulembera.
 
Ne bamalayika 
Nga bakuba-ennanga,
Era ne Katonda,
Mukama w'obulamu-.
 
Namuwammantira 
Mu nzikiza-ey'ensi;
Yanzaawula,era 
Mu kufa-alimponya.
