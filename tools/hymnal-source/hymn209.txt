OBWAKABAKA BWO BUJJE
OBWAKABAKA bwo bujje
Kaakano tukyogedde:
Yesu onjigirize 
-Okukisabira ddala.
 
Yesu,sooka oyigire 
Muno mu mwoyo gwange:
Naakusembeza gye ndi,
Ongobemu ebibi.
 
Ombeerenga bulijjo
Mbuulire-ekigambo kyo;
Olyoke ojje nate,
Mu mwoyo gya bannange.

Ebiro bwe biriggwaawo-;
Kitaawo bye yalaga,
Olijja n'ettendo lyo,
N'olya-obwakabaka bwo
