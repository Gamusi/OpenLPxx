AYI YESU MUKAMA
AYI Yesu Mukama,
Onsonyiwe-ebibi,
Omponye-okwegomba kw'ensi:
Mbeere mulongonfu.

Ayi Yesu Mukama,
Ommalemu okutya,
Mbeere omuddu wo-akwagala,
Ndyoke ntuuke gy'oli.

Ayi Yesu Mukama,
Tomganya kuwaba;
Mu nzikiza nga ssiraba;
Mulisa-ekkubo lyo.

Ayi Yesu Mukama,
Ntuusa mu ssanyu lyo;
Mbeerenga eyo mu ggulu,
Emirembe gyonna.
