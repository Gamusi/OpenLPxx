YESU MUSUMBA WANGE
YESU Musumba wange,
Bulijjo ansanyusa;
Yesu anjagala,ammanyi,
Byonna-ebirungi abimpa;
Ampita-erinnya lyange,
Ambeera n'ekisa kye.

Ntambula nga sikyatya,
Yesu Mukuumi wange;
Mu biro-eby'enjala-andiisa,
Era bwe nnumwa ennyonta,
Antwala ku mabbali
Ag'amazzi-amateefu.
 
Si kirungi nsanyuke?
Si kirungi njaguze?
Ne bwe ndifa talindeka 
Alintuusa ne mu ggulu,
Eyo gye naabeeranga,
Mu maaso ga Katonda.
