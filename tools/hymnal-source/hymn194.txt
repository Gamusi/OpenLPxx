YESU EKISA KYO-EKINGI
YESU ekisa kyo-ekingi 
Kinjiike ku mwoyo,
Guleme-okukyama nate 
Gunywerere ku ggwe.

Omuliro gwa Katonda 
Gwake mu nze leero;
Gwokye-amasengere gonna,
Guntukuze-omwoyo.

Eyakka-edda ku baddu bo,
Omwoyo,kka ku nze;
Omwana wo nkukoowoola,
Ebyonoono-obyokye.

Omuliro-ogutukuza,
Gunjiike ku mwoyo:
Leeta-omnusana n'obulamu,
Byake bintukuze.
 
Ne ndyoka nfuna-amaayi go,
Obutasirika;
-Obugagga bwange,Mukama,
-Obulamu bwange-era.
