ALERUUYA MYIMBE
ALERUUYA myimbe,
Leero Yesu-azuukidde,
Tuyimbe nga twebaza 
Olw'okuwangula kwe.
Aleruuya 

Obulumi bw'okufa,
N'amaanyi gaakwo gonna,
Byonna abiwangudde:
Naffe ka tujaguze.
Aleruuya

Naffe-abaali-abasibe 
Mu buddu obwekibi 
Leero naffe tusinza:
Ssetaani awanguddwa.
Aleruuya

Ka tujaguze fenna 
Kubanga yawangula;
Ka tuyimbe-amatendo 
Kuba tuli ba ddembe.
Aleruuya.
