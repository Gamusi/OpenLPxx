MU BIRO-EBY'ENNAKU
MU biro-eby'ennaku 
Yesu-onnyambenga 
Nneme-okugwa wansi,
Nga nkwerabidde.
Bwe mba mbuusabuusa,
Ontunuulire,
Ompanirirenga 
Olw'amaanyi go.

Enkwe za Ssetaani 
Ze zinnumba-ennyo,
Obugagga-obw'ensi 
Businza-omwoyo 
Ka njijukireggwe 
Eyafiirira:
Ggwe-oli kigo kyange 
Mwe neekweka nze.
 
Yesu bw'onoobanga 
Onkangavvula,
Byonna-ebijja ku nze 
Ka mbikkirize;
Neewaayo nze gy'oli 
Ndi wuwo wekka,
-Ontukuze,ombeere,
Ennaku zonna.

Mu ntalo-ez'entiisa 
Nkwesiga wekka 
Bwe ndinafuwala,
Ojjanga gye ndi;
Omubiri gwange 
Ne bwe gulifa,
-Onzuukize mu bafu 
Nze-akweyabiza.
