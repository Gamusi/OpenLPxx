TWEYANZIZA-EKISA KYO
TWEYANZIZA-ekisa kyo 
Okututuusa fenna,
Mu mwaka guno-omuggya
Ayi Kitaffe, wulira. 

Mu bunafu n'ennaku, 
Ka tuddukire gy'oli 
Ng'ekkubo litubula; 
Yesu-otukulembere.

Bwe tulisemberera 
Ekiwonvu-eky'okufa, 
-Omuggo gwo n'oluga lwo 
Bye biritusanyusa.

Bulijjo tubeerenga 
Beesigwa,balongoofu;
Ka tugumiikirize,
Otusaayize-engule.

Mu nsi ey'omu ggulu,
Tulikuba-ennanga-empya 
Nga tukusuuta wekka,
Ggwe-afuga-abantu bonna
