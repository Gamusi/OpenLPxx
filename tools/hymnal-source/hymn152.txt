WEEBAZE GGWE EMMEEME YANGE
WEEBAZE ggwe-emmeeme yange,
eebaze mwoyo gwange;
-Olw'ekitiibwa kye ekingi-ennyo,
Leeta-ettndo lyo lyonna;
Aleruuya,Aleruuya!
Weebaze Katonda wo. 

Weebaze-ekisa kye kingi 
Eri bajjajja-ab'edda.
-Olw'ekisa kye-ekitakoma,
Eyaliwo edda n'edda.
Aleruuya,Aleruuya!
Weebaze Katonda wo. 

Ye Kitaffe,atukuuma,
Atuliisa bulijjo
Amanyi-obunafu bwaffe,
Amanyi-obunafu bwaffe,
Atuwonya mu kabi.
Aleruuya,Aleruuya!
Weebaze Katonda wo. 

Bamalayika be bonna 
Bayimba ettendo lye,
Oyanguwe-ojje n'ebire 

Olye-obwakabaka bwo.
Aleruuya,Aleruuya!
Weebaze Katonda wo.
