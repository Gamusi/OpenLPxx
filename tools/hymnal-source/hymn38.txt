ABASUMBA BAALI BAKUUMA
-ABASUMBA baali bakuuma, 
-Endiga zaabwe-ekiro, 
Malayika n'akka ku nsi 
N'eyakaayakana.

Mmwe-abasumba temwekanga 
Ndeese-amawulire 
-Ag'essanyu lingi nnyo nnyini 
Eri-abantu bonna.

Leero mu kyalo kya Dawudi 
Era mu kika kye,
Muzaaliddwa-Omulokozi,
Yesu lye linnya lye.

Omwana munaamulaba
Ng'azazikiddwa-eri 
Ng'abikkiddwa mu bugoye 
Mu kiraalo ky'ente.

Amangu ne walabika,
Mu ggulu ne ku nsi, 
Eggye-erya bamalayika 
Ne bayimba bati: 

Katonda -aweebwe-ekitiibwa 
Mu ggulu ne mu nsi:
-Emirembe gibe mu bantu 
Bonna-abasiimibwa.
