MU MYAKA SI MINGI
MU myaka si mingi,
Ebbanga si ddene,
Tulyebaka-awamu n'abo
Abali mu ntaana.
Kye nvudde nsaba ggwe
Eyatufiirira,
Teekateeka-omwoyo gwange,
So tondekanga-eno

Obulamu bwaffe,
Bunnatera okuggwaawo,
Olunaku lusembera,
Olw'omusango gwo.
Mulokozi wange,
Onziggyeko-ebibi,
Onnaaze mu musaayi gwo,
Onsembeze gy'oli.

Wakyasigaddeyo 
Ennaku si nnyingi,
Naffe tulituuka eyo
Gye katajja kabi.
Mukama w'obulamu
Ompolerezenga
Nkwesiga okundokola
Mu byonoono byonna.

Ewaffe si wano
Naye-eri mu ggulu,
Yesu-tumulindirira,
Alituuka mangu.
Mununuzi wange,
Tunuulira bwe ndi,
Siyinza-awatali ggwe
Kugumiikiriza.

Kale tujjukire
Ebyawandiikibwa,
Kaakano ekiro kiyise,
Emmambya esaze
Ka twambule-ebibi
Ebyatusanyusa,
Ffe twambale-ebibi
Ebyatusanyusa,
Ffe twambale-obutukuvu,
Mukama waffe ajja.
