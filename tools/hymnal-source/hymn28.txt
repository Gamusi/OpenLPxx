YESU ALIJJA N'EBIRE
YESU alijja n'ebire
Eyafiirira-abanyu;
Abatukuvu be bangi
Wamu naye bayimba,
Aleruuya!
Ajja wansi-okufuga.
 
Bonna baliraba Yesu
Ng'ali mu kitiibwa kye:
N'abo-abaamuyigganya 
N'abo-abaamufumita
Balikaaba,
Yesu bw'alirabika.

Wansi ne waggulu wonna
N'ebirimu biriggwaawo-:
Abaamulyamu olukwe
Baliwulira Yesu
Bw'alisala,
Gubasinze,muveewo.
 
Abeegendereza bonna
Abamulindirira 
Balitwalibwa mu bbanga,
Balisangayo Yesu.
Eyeewayo,
Okuleeta-obulamu.

Bonna bonna bakusinza 
Yesu, mu kitiibwa kyo;
Jjangu,Mulokozi waffe
Olye-Obwakabaka bwo
Tukusuute;
Jjangu,Mukama waffe.
