YESU MUKAMA WAFFE
YESU Mukama waffe,
Otugumiikirize,
Twetoowazizza leero.

Yesu ggwe-Omutukuvu 
Otugonze ffe-emyoyo 
Nga tukyalina-ebbanga.

Otujjuze ffe-Omwoyo 
Omubeezi ow'ekisa,
Tukwatenga-amazima.

Ku lw'entuuyo ez'omusaayi,
N'amaziga go gonna;
Ge watonnyesa mu nsi.

Ku lw'okwagala-okungi 
Okwakutufiiriza,
Yesu otuwulire
