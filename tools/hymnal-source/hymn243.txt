MUJJE MWENNA-ABAKOOYE
MUJJE mwenna-abakooye 
Nze naabawummuza:
-Eddoboozi nga lya kisa
Ery'Omulokozi:
Litutegeeza bw'ali
Ow'ekisa kyonna,
Bw'ayagala-okuwonya 
Emyoyo gy'abantu 
 
Mujje mwenna-abakyamye
Nze naabamulisa:
-Eddoboozi nga lya ssanyu 
Mu nzikiza-ekutte.
Mu nsiko ey'omu nsi
Twali tuwabye nnyo,
Mu kkubo lye yatuzza,
Ffe ne tusanyuka
 
Mujje mwenna-abafudde 
Naabawa obulamu-:
-Eddoboozi lye lya manyi,
Lituzzaamu-omwoyo.
Entalo zaffe nzibu;
-Omulabe mukambwe;
Amaanyi g'okulwana
Gava eri Yesu.
 
Era-anajjanga gye ndi
Nze siimugobenga:
-Eddoboozi lya Mukama 
Lye litugumya-ennyo:
Ekisa-ekyenkanaawo
Tewali-asaanidde,
Ye ye-atusembezezza
Kale tumwebaze
