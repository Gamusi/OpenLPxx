MMWE MWENNA ABANOONYA-OKUTUUKA MU GGULU 
MMWE mwenna abanoonya-okutuuka mu ggulu,
Mulabe ku Mukama,mubeere n'essanyu;
Tewali kkubo ddala,bwe mutayingira 
Mu luggi lw'omu ggulu olwalagirirwa.

Oluggi olwo ye Yesu-eyatugamba nti:
Buli akkiriza nze asonyiyibwa-ebibi;
Mmwe mwenna abakooye,kale mujje gye ndi 
Nammwe aboonoonyi be najja-okulokola.
 
Leero terunnaggalwa oluggi lw'eggulu;
Lukyali-awo luggule okuyingiramu.
Kale tolwa,yanguwa okuyita omwo;
Katonda-akugamba ntiKale jjangu leero.
