EWALA MU BUYUDAAYA
EWALA mu Buyudaaya,
Eriyo-olusozi,
Kwe yatufiirira Yesu,
Ye Mukama waffe.

Ffe-abantu be tetumanyi 
Ennaku bwe zaali,
Ze yalaba ku lw'abantu,
-Omulokozi Yesu.

Yafa ffe tufune-eddembe 
Atuwe n'obulamu:
Yafa atutuuse-ewuwe 
Gy'atudde mu ggulu.

Tewali-eyandiyinzizza 
Kulokola munne,
Newankubadde ye yekka 
Kusinga-omusango.

Mukama y'ayinza yekka 
Okutuggulira 
Oluggi olw'omu ggulu,
N'okutuyingiza.

Tusaanidde ffe twagale
Mukama waffe-oyo;
Tweweeyo-okumuweereza 
Eyatufiirira
