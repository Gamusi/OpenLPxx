MMWE-ENSOZI-EMPANVU-ENNYO
MMWE-ensozi-empanvu-ennyo,
Mujaguze mwenna! 
-Ensenyi n'ebiwonvu,
Mumutendereze.
Muwulire-eddoboozi lye 
Libuulira-eby'okujja kwe.

Kale mmwe-ebizinga 
Ebiri mu nnyanja;
Empewo-ennyingi-ennyo 
Zikuntira ku mmwe;
Zirongoosa ekkubo lye 
Kabaka ow'ekitiibwa.

Mmwe-amawanga mwenna,
Muzuukuke mangu;
-Abantu bammwe bonna 
Beefunire-eddembe 
Ery'Omwana wa Katonda 
Ly'abaleetera kaakano.

Amawanga gonna 
Agali-ewala ennyo,
Agatamanyanga 
Mulokozi w'ensi;
Kale,mwenna mujje gy'ali,
Oyo eyakomererwa.

Mwenna muyimbe nnyo,
Nga mugenda gy'ali;
Mwenna mukumgaane 
-Okuva mu nsi zonna;
Tuyingire mu kibuga,
Ekyaffe,-eky'olubeerera.
