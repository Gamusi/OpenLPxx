TULINA KABAKA WAFFE
TULINA Kbaka waffe 
Eyava mu ggulu:
Kale fenna abaana be 
Tumugoberere.

Okwagala kwe kungi nnyo 
Eri-abantu bonna;
Naffe twagale bannaffe
Nga bwe kiyinzika.
 
Yesu tayagala kibi 
Na kwerowoozaako;
Ye omulabe we y'ani,
Amulyamu-olukwe?
 
Ayagala tulongoose 
Emibiri gyaffe;
Abatali batukuvu 
Tebaliba naye.

Tuli kibiina kitono,
Naye tusaanidde 
Okutwalira Katonda 
-Emyoyo-emirongoofu.
