AYI YESU-OW'EKISA
AYI Yesu-ow'ekisa 
Ggwe bulamu-obw'abantu,
Omutonzi wa byonna,
Otuwulire.
 
Ebibi bwe byabunya 
Ku bantu bonna-okufa 
Ggwe eyabalokola,
Otusonyiwe.

Ggwe-eyalinnya mu ggulu 
-Okulya-obwakabaka bwo,
Mukama w'abakama,
Otuwulire.

Ggwe-olikomawo nate 
Okusala-omusango 
Gw'abalamu n'abafu,
Otulokole
