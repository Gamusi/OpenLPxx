MU BIRO-EBY'EMPEWO
MU biro-eby'empewo
E Beesirekemu;
Muwala omulongoofu 
Yazaala-Omwana we.
 
Musanyuke mwenna 
Muyimbe-ettendo lye;
Mujaguze, mujaguze,
Yesu azaaliddwa.
 
Yesu azaaliddwa,
Mu kisa kye-ekingi 
Eggye erya bamalayika 
Batendereza nnyo.

Abasumba bazze 
-Okusinza Omwana-oyo;
Abalaguzi batona,
Mugavu ne zaabu.

Ne bamalayika 
Baase-okulamusa
Omwana-oyo mu kisibo 
Ye Mukama waffe. 

Azze-okununula, 
-Okuba -Omulokozi;
Azze- okuwangula-okufa,
Tasingika maanyi.

Azze n'okwagala,
-Okuleeta-omukisa; 
Eri abantu be bonna, 
Atuyigirize.

Oli mu kiraalo 
Ggwe-eyava mu ggulu,
Amaggwa galifumita 
Ekyenyi kyo, Yesu.

Naye ggwe-otwagaza,
-Ebigambo by'obulamu;
Era-otuyinzisa fenna 
-Okukugoberera

Ka tumuweereze 
Nga bamalayika;
Kale tusanyuke fenna 
Azaaliddwa leero
