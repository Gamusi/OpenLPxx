KATONDA EYANTONDA NZE
KATONDA eyantonda nze 
Mu mubiri-omwangu;
Ndyoke nnyinze-okusobola 
-Okutambula wonna;
Singa mbuliddwa-amagezi,
Naye mujjukira 
Mu mutima ogw'essanyu 
Ng'omwana omuto.
 
Yesu Kabaka, Mukama;
Ompe-ekitala kyo 
Ekyangu era ekyogi 
Nnwanyise-abalabe;
Ndyoke mbawangule abo,
Era nkuweereze 
-Emisana era n'ekiro,
Mbeerenga-omusajja.

Omwoyo ogw'amazima 
Oguwa-omubiri 
-Essanyu mu kifo ky'ennyombo,
-Omusana-omulungi;
Amagezi nga gambuze 
Ojje embeerenga;
Ompe ekirabo kino 
-Otwale-omwoyo gwange.
