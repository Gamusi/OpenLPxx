SIYINZA N'AKATONO
SIYINZA n'akatono 
Yesu nga toliiwo;
Okulwana-akaseera 
Mu maaso g'ekibi;
Abalabe bange bonna 
Bantaayiza bubi;
Omulokozi wange,
Ombeere n'amaanyi.

Nze ndi munafu ddala.
Amaanyi gampweddemu;
So sirina magezi,
Ekkubo limbuze.
Beera-Omusaale wange,
Mu masamganzira,
Bw'onkwata n'omukono 
Ndituuka-emirembe.
 
Siyinza n'akatono,
Kutambula nzekka;
Ggwe-oli Mubeezi wange 
Mu biro-eby'ennaku:
Ggwe tonjabuliranga,
Nneme-okubungeeta,
Siritya kabi konna,
Mu ntuuko-ez'okufa
