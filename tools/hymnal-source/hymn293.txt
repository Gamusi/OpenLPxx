KAAKANO TWEBAZA
KAAKANO twebaza,
Katonda-ow'emirembe 
Eyatukolera 
Ebitusanyusa ffe;
Okuva mu buto
Yatuwa omukisa;
Era-ebirabo bye 
Tebitegeezeka.

Katonda ow'ekisa 
Weemale-emyoyo gyaffe;
Tuwe-emirembe gyo 
Beeranga kumpi naffe,
Otulung'amyenga
Mu kkubo ly'obulamu;
Ffe naawe mu ggulu.
tusanyuke fenna.

Kitaffe,n'Omwana,
N'-Omwoyo Omutukuvu,
