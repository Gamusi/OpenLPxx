MUWULIRE MMWE-ABANTU BE
MUWULIRE mmwe-abantu be
Abamukiriza;
Mubunye ebigambo bye
Kristo wa Katonda.
 
Mubuulire wonna wonna,
Mu mawanga gonna;
Banabbi ye gwe baalanga 
Ye musana gw'ensi.
 
Katonda-ow'olubeerera,
Eyeefuula omuntu,
Eyatwala omubiri,
Ye Katonda ddala.

Katonda-ava mu Kitaffe 
Asinzibwa bonna,
Eyatuwonya mu kufa 
N'atufuula abaana.
 
Fenna abamukkiriza
Atuwe-omwoyo gwe;
Bwe tuba tataataagana,
Ye atulumgamya
