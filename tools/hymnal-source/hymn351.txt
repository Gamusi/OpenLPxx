KATONDA WANJAGALA
KATONDA wanjagala 
N'ompa-Omwana wo Yesu 
Abe mukwano gwange 
Annyambe mu bizibu.

Yesu tolekangayo 
-Okuba mukwano gwange;
Bulijjo beera nange 
Era n'okunnumggamya.

Naakwagala ntya Yesu?
Nze naakutonera ki?
Siraba kye mba nkuwa 
Anti byonna bintu byo.

Naye kye nnina kimu 
Nkuwadde-omwoyo gwange;
Naakugobereranga,
Ondagirire-ekkubo.
