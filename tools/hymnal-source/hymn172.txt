OKWAGALA OKUTAGGWAAWO
OKWAGALA-okutaggwaawo 
Okw'olubeerera,
Kukulukuta n'amaanyi,
Ge mazzi agataggwaawo-.

Ffe tetuyinza kulinnya,
Mukama Kristo-akke:
Ye abeera wonna-wonna,
Tewali w'abula.
 
Naye ye Mubeezi waffe 
-Atubeera bulijjo;
Alabwa mu kizikiza 
Ne mu kwagala-era.
 
Ebyambalo bye byawonya,
Endwadde z'abantu;
tumweyune bwe tulumwa,
Tuwonere ddala.
 
-Okwagala-okutagambika,
-Erinnya lyo liwonya,
Okukugaana kwe kufa,
Ggwe-olina obulamu-
