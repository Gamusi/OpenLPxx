KATONDA-EYANNONDA NZE
KATONDA-eyannonda nze 
Si lwa bulungi bwange,
Eyannonda-obulonzi
N'anzigyako ebibi.
Nnyinza ntya-okumalayo 
-Ebbanja lyange-eryenkanaawo-?

Yanzigya mu balabe,
N'anfuula omwana we;
Yanzigya mu bujeemu
N'anfuula-omutukuvu;
Nnyinza ntya-okumalayo 
-Ebbanja lyange-eryenkanaawo-?

Bwe ndituuka mu ggulu 
Olw'obutuukirivu;
Obwa Yesu,si bwange,
Bwe ndiraba-amaaso ge;
Ne ndyoka ntegeereraawo-
-Ebbanja bwe litaggwaayo.
