BATUULA MU GGULU
BATUULA mu ggulu 
Wamu naawe 
Abatuukirivu;
Basanyuka.
Bayimba bulijjo,
Tweyanze ekisa kyo,
Ggwe-eyatwagala-ennyo,
Mulokozi.

-Omusaayi gwa Yesu 
Gwanaaza nnyo;
Ebyambalo e'bya-
Batukuvu.
Kyebava batuula 
Wamu naawe,Katonda;
Kyebava bayimba 
Ettendo lyo.

Ani ayagala 
-Okutuuka-eyo?
Jjangu Yesu gy'ali;
Yanguwako.
Anaakusonyiwa,
Taaleme kukkiriza,
Nga bwe yasuubiza,
Mukama wo.

Tasingika ddala 
Ekisa kye:
Alibalokola 
Abantu be. 
Asangula-ebibi 
N'omusaayi-ogw'amaanyi:
Asinga obubi 
Taamuleme.
