OBWAKABAKA BWO
OBWAKABAKA bwo,
Yesu,bujje mu nsi;
Menya n'omuggo gwo,
Obuddu bw'ekibi.

Twetaaga-emirembe
N'obutuukirivu 
-Okukyawagananga
-Otumalemu mangu.

Ebyasuubizibwa 
Bye tulindirira;
Bituukirizibwe,
Ayi Yesu Mukama.
 
Tukwegayiridde,
Okutusaasira;
Tukemgentererwa,
Jjangu otubeere.
 
Enzikiza-ekutte 
Ebuna-ensi zonna;
Musana gw'obulamu,
Yaka-obutaggwaawo.
