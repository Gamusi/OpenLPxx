EWALA MU GGULU
EWALA mu ggulu 
Bamalayika be 
Bayimba n'essanyu 
Nga batendereza-
Aleruuya!
Basanyuka okuyimba 
Aleruuya!

Era mu nsi muno 
Katonda-akkiriza 
Ennyimba z'abato 
Bonna-abamwagala.
Aleruuya!
Naffe ka tumuyimbire,
Aleruuya!

Yesu Mulokozi,
Tukwegayiridde 
Otuyingirize 
Amazima gonna.
Aleruuya!
Tulyoke tukuyimbire,
Aleruuya!

Ayi Yesu Mukama,
Bunya-ekigambo kyo;
Buli ggwanga-ery'ensi
Likyukire gy'oli.
Aleruuya!
-Ebika byonna biriyimba,
Aleruuya!
