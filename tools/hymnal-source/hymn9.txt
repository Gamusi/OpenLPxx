GY'OLI YESU TUWAAYO
GY'OLI Yesu tuwaayo
Olunaku lwo luno;
Ggwe wekka obimanyi nnyo;
Tuleme-okulwonoona,
Otuwe-emikisa gyo.

Bwe lunaaleeta-essanyu,
Mubeezi waffe jjangu,
Tuleme-okutegebwa
Mu nkwe z'omulyolyomi;
Tuli mu lukoola ffe,
Omulabe-omugobe.
 
Twagala kino kyokka
Okusiimibwa Yesu,
Bw'anaaba akomyewo
Okutuyita leero,
Atusange ffe fenna
Nga tumulindirira.
