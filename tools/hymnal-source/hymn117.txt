TUSANYUSE-OKUJJA MU MAASO GO 
TUSANYUSE-okujja mu maaso go,
Tusuubiza leero mu maaso go;
Tubeere-abaana bo-ennaku zonna,
Okukuweereza n'obwesigwa.

Naye tumanyidde ddala kino 
Nti tetuyinza awatali ggwe;
Naye ggwe watusuubiza kino 
Okubeeranga naffe bulijjo.

Tumanyi nti ggwe oli mwesigwa,
Toyinza kutuleka mu kkubo;
Kale-essubi eryo litugumye 
Liryoke litutuuse mu ggulu.

Otuyise okukuweereza 
Tuwaayo-obulamu bwaffe bwonna;
Tukuweereze nga bw'osaanira 
Olyoke-oweebwe-ekitiibwa mu nsi
