GGWE MUSUMBA-OMULUNGI
GGWE Musumba-omulungi 
Ggwe-okuuma endiga zo;
Tewali-ekiriyinza 
Kuzikusikulako.
 
-Omulokozi wawaayo 
-Obulamu bwo ku lwaffe;
Mu ngalo zo mulimu 
-Enkovu z'emisumaali.
 
Naakutenderezanga 
Nga mpulira by'omggamba;
Ndyoke nfaanane ng'abo 
B'oli nabo mu ggulu.

Ayi-Musumba-omulungi 
Beeranga kumpi nange,
Njige eddoboozi lyo,
Nkwatenga ekkubo lyo.

Naakugobereranga 
Buli wonna w'oyita;
Olintuusa mu ggulu 
Mu maaso ga Kitaffe.
