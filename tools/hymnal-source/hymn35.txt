ABAKRISTAAYO BOONA-AB'OMU NSI
ABAKRISTAAYO bonna-ab'omu nsi
Mugolokoke leero n'essanyu 
Mutendereze Omulokozi 
Eyazaalirwa-e Beesirekemu:
Bamalayika be be baasooka 
Okutendereza Omwana oyo.

Bo baasooka okukibuulira 
Abasumba-abo abawombeefu;
Nti temutya,kubanga-olwa leero 
Azaaliddwa-Omulokozi wammwe:
Leero Katonda kye yasuubiza 
Kituukiridde: Yesu ye azze. 

Awo ekibiina ne kirabika 
Eggye lyonna-erya bamalayika 
Nga bayimba oluyimba-olugya, 
Nga batendereza okwagal 
Okwamuleeta okuzaalibwa 
N'obuwombeefu obwenkanidde-awo.

Nabo bwe baamala-okuwulia
Amangu ago ne bagolokoka, 
Ne bagendayo mbiro nnyingi nnyo 
Balabe Katonda kye yakola;
Ne basanga Omwana we Yesu, 
Ne bamusinza n'essanyu lingi.

Naffe twanguwe-okugulumiza 
Ekisa-ekingi-eky'Omulokozi;
Tujjukirenga-ekisa kye kyonna 
Ekyamuleeta-okutufiirira; 
Bwotutyo bwe tunaamuyimbira 
Kabaka waffe-ow'olubeerera
