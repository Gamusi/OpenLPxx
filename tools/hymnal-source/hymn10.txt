OMPULIRE Kitaffe
Ggwe Mukuumi wange:
Beera kumpi nange;
Onjijanjabenga.

Ayi Mukaama Yesu,
Atuwa-obulamu,
Ka nziruke-embiro
Okutuuka gy'oli.
    
Ggwe Mubeezi waffe,
Mwoyo wa Katonda,
Laba bwe nkwetaaga
Nsembeza, nnongoosa.
 
Jjangu, Ayi Katonda;
Tondekanga wano,
Okuva kaakano,
Tuula mu nda yange.
