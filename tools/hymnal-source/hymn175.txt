YESU,SSANYU LYANGE
YESU,ssanyu lyange
Ddundiro ly'omwoyo 
Ka nkusinze ggwe!
Ennaku z'omwoyo 
Ze zimpaliriza 
Ndi wuwo naawe wange,
Nkwagala-okusinga bonna 
Ggwe Mukama wange!
 
Olw'okukuuma kwo 
Abalabe bange 
Banafu gyendi;
Ssetaani bw'annumba,
Nsaba-ompe amaanyi,
Tombeera wala!
Neetoolooleddwa-obubi 
N'okufa kwa ntiisa, naye 
Yesu ggwe onkuuma.
 
Ka tugonze-emyoyo,
Ka tumuwe-ekifo 
Omusanyusa.
Yesu k'atufuge,
Tumuwulirenga,
Tujjule-essanyu 
Mu buyinike bwonna:
Atuwenga-essanyu lino,
Okubeera naffe.
