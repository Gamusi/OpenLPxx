OMUTAMBUZE-OMUTO NZE
-OMUTAMBUZE-omuto nze 
Natandise leero;
Nnumggamya okumala 
-Olugendo lwo lwonna.

Mulwanyi wo-omuto nze,
Atalina maanyi;
Mbeeranga ggwe-ow'amaanyi 
Nnwanenga n'ebibi.

Omwana wo-omto nze,
Nkweyabiza wekka;
Mulokozi,ompenga
-Ekisa kyo kye nsabye.

Mmanyi bwe ndi-omunafu 
Naye ntunuulira;
Ompe-okukuweereza 
Leero ne bulijjo.
