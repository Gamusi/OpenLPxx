YESU EYASOOKA
YESU eyasooka 
Okutwagala;
Saasira-obunafu 
Bwaffe-abaddu bo.
 
Yesu ggwe muteefu 
Ggwe muwombeefu;
N'obuvumu bungi,
Ffe tujja gy'oli.

Naye tuli bayi,
Ffe tuyidde nnyo:
Era-ebibi byaffe
Bituyinze nnyo.

Ggwe Ayinza-byonna,
Otuwe-amaanyi 
Okuwangulanga 
Mu kukemebwa.
 
Tuli bagayaavu;
Ggwe munyiikivu 
Tuwe-fenna-amaanyi,
Okunyiikira.

Tuli banafu nnyo,
Ggwe-oli wa maanyi;
Otunywerezenga 
Mu mazima go.
 
Ggwe mubeezi waffe,
Otuzibire 
Eri omulabe 
Eri omulabe 
Waffe Ssetaani.
 
Tukweyanza Yesu,
Ggwe-atujuna-ennyo:
Otuwe Omwoyo 
Omutukuvu.
