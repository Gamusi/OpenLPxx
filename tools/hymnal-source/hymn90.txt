YASULA MU NTAANA, MUKAMA WAFFE
YASULA mu ntaana,Mukama waffe 
Ng'alinda obudde,-Omulokozi.
Yazuukira mu bafu,ng'abawangudd(e) abalabe 
Yava mu magombe ng'omwanguzi 
N'abeer(a) omulam(u) emirembe gyonna
Yeebale! Yeebale!
Yeebale! yazuukira.
