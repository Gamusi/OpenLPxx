MWENNA MUSANYUKE
MWENNA musanyuke 
Yesu ye Kabaka;
Ebitonde byonna,
Biyimbe n'essanyu:
Muyimuse emitiima,
Musanyuke,mujaguze.
 
Yesu-Omulokozi,
Ye-afuga n'ekisa;
Eyatufiirira 
Ye-atudde ku ntebe;
Muyimuse emitiima,
Musanyuke,mujaguze.
 
Obwakabaka bwe 
Bwe butaliggwaawo;
Alina-obuyinza,
Mu nsi ne mu ggulu.
Muyimuse emitiima,
Musanyuke,mujaguze.
 
Ali ku mukono 
Gwa ddyo gwa Katonda;
-Abalabe be bonna 
Balikungubaga.
Muyimuse emitiima,
Musanyuke,mujaguze.
