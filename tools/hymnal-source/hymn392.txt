SIMANYI BINABAAWO
Simanyi binabaawo,
Katonda abinkweka
Ennaku ze ndiraba-edda
Ankweka lwa kisa;
N'essanyu ly'atusuubiza
Lye lituwoomera

Ng'enda gy'antwala yonna
Mwesiga bwesizi!
Sibuusabuusa so sitya;
Kubanga ye amanyi.

Obutamanya obwo 
Bwe nsinga okwagala;
Ankute n'omukono gwe
Yesu-annywezeza
Ampummuza buwummuza,
Kubanga mwesiga.

Ng'enda gy'antwala yonna
Mwesiga bwesizi!
Sibuusabuusa so sitya;
Kubanga ye amanyi.

Ka ntambule ne Yesu
Yonna gy'aba-antwala
Awali ye mu nzikiza
Nyinza okulaba
Era muzibe-amwesiga
Tayinza kubula.

Ng'enda gy'antwala yonna
Mwesiga bwesizi!
Sibuusabuusa so sitya;
Kubanga ye amanyi.
