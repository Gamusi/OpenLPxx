ZUUKUKA MWOYO GWANGE
ZUUKUKA mwoyo gwange,
Katonda-akusembeza,
Taakugobere bweru,
Akwagalira ddala.

Gw'osaba ye Kabaka 
Omuyinza wa byonna,
-Okumukooyesa teri 
Olw'ekisa kye-ekingi.

Yesu,sooka-ontikkule 
Omugugu ogw'ebibi,
Ogunzitoowerera,
Gwe sijja kusobola.

Mu musaayi-ogwayiika
Edda ku musaalaba;
Ku lw'abalina-ebibi,
Yesu,-onnaalize ddala.
 
Nkooye nnyo,ompummuze 
Weemale-omwoyo gwange,
Njagala-Omulokozi 
Anammalamu-ebibi.

Bwe ntyo bwe nkuyimbira 
Bulijjo ettendo lyo;
Abakwesiga wekka,
Baliraba-ekisa kyo
