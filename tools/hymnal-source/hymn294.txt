EKISA KYA YESU
EKISA kya Yesu,
Kisinga-obulungi:
Kiki-ekyamutufiiriza?
Ekisa kye kingi.

Yesu ku muti,kwe yanfiirira;
Yesu ye yandokola;neeyanz(a) ekisa kye.
 
Mu kisa neeraba 
Nze nga nnina-ebibi;
Era kyennaavanga nteeka 
Byonna Yesu gy'ali.

Yesu ku muti,kwe yanfiirira;
Yesu ye yandokola;neeyanz(a) ekisa kye.
 
Mu kisa nafuna,
-Omutima omuggya;
Kyenvudde nsaba bulijjo 
-Okusiimwa Katonda.

Yesu ku muti,kwe yanfiirira;
Yesu ye yandokola;neeyanz(a) ekisa kye.
 
Mukama,yongera 
Okumpa-ekisa kyo;
Era nze bwe ntyo nnyongere 
-Okukwagala ennyo.

Yesu ku muti,kwe yanfiirira;
Yesu ye yandokola;neeyanz(a) ekisa kye.
