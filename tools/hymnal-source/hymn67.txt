ABANTU ABAABEERANGA
ABANTU abaabeeranga 
Mu nzikiza y'ensi 
Omusana gubaakidde
Okugoba-okufa.

Watutikkula-emigugu
N'omenya-omujoozi,
Nga bwe wakola-omulabe,
Ku lwa Midiyaani.

Anaayitibwanga-erinnya 
Kabaka-ow'amaanyi,
Kabaka ow'emirembe,
Katonda-omwagalwa,

Jjangu Enjuba-Entukuvu 
Mulisa-ensi zonna;
Bonna bajjule-essanyu lyo;
Mu mitima gyabwe.

Omwana atuzaaliddwa 
-Omwana-atuweereddwa;
-Obuyinza bwe bulifuga,
Mu ggulu ne mu nsi.

Buyinza bwe bulibuna 
Mu mawanga gonna;
N'ensala ze ez'ensonga,
Za mirembe gyonna.

Kale Yesu,jjangu leero 
Wamu ne Kitaffe;
N'Omwoyo Omutukuvu 
Tufugire ddala
