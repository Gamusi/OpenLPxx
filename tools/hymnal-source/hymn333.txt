OMUKWANO GWA YESU
OMUKWANO gwa Yesu
Tegutegeerekeka;
Omwoyo-Omutukuvu 
Y'anjigiriza yekka;
Emirembe gijuza 
Bwe gityo-omwoyo gwange,
Era gikulukuta Bulijjo-okunsanyusa.

Byonna-ebitalabika 
Kaakano mbirengedde;
Bibikkuliddwa gye ndi 
Ku lw'ekisa kya Yesu;
Era-ebinafu by'ensi 
Katonda yabironda,
Alyoke-akwase-ensonyi 
Eby'amaanyi mangi nnyo.

Eby'entiisa sibitya;
Nvumbudde-ekiwummulo;
Sikyayengetana,so 
Sikyataagana nate.
Nnina-ennono y'obulamu,
Kwe kukkiriza Yesu,
Era nnyimba n'essanyu 
Ye Mulokozi wange.
