NEEWAAYO MU MIKONO
NEEWAAYO mu mikono 
Gya Yesu kaakano;
Essanyu lyo linkutte,
Sirema kwewaayo.
 
Omwana wa Katonda,
Yesu anjagala;
Neewaayo gy'oli wekka,
N'ebyange-era byonna.

Mukama wange,jjangu
Fuga-omwoyo guno;
Tegukyali ku bwange;
Gugwo wekka leero.
 
Kabaka wange-ontwale 
Ne bye nnina byonna;
Onkuume n'ekisa kyo,
Mbeerenga mwesigwa.
