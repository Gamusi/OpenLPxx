TUSAASIRE FFE-ABANAKU
TUSAASIRE ffe-abanaku,
Tuzirise olw'ebibi;
Otubeere-Omulokozi
Kubanga ggwe osaasira.

Tusonyiwe abeeneya,
Tutambule mu maaso go 
Ng'aboonoonyi-abasonyiwe,
Tukwebaze-Omulokozi.
 
Tuzze gy'oli-Omulokozi,
Ggwe eyafa ku lw'abantu 
Naffe fenna aboonoonyi 
Tumanyi nti osaasira. 

Tetukyatya omulabe,
Takyalina maanyi gonna:
Kale leka tukwesige,
Tusaasire -Omulokozi
