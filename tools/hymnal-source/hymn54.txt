NSANYUKIRA OLUYIMBA
NSANYUKIRA-oluyimba
Lwa bamalayika 
Lwe baayimba okulanga 
Okujja kwa Yesu.
Nze sirina bulungi,
Nnina-ebibi bingi;
Naye yakka ku lwange 
Yesu Mulokozi.

Mmanyi Mukama waffe 
Yafuuka omuto,
-Okulaga empisa ennungi 
Ffe tuzikwatenga 
Era bwe naafubanga 
Okumufaanana 
Ye tanneerabirenga,
Kubanga-anjagala.

Siyinza kumulaba 
Kaakano n'amaaso 
Naye ennyimba zange 
Anaaziwwulira.
Bwe naamugonderanga,
Alintuusa-ewuwe 
Ne mutenderezanga 
Nga bwe kisaanidde.
