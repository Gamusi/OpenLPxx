 KA tutambulenga nabo 
 Abanoonya erinnya lyo,
 Era n'abatukuvu bo 
 Emirembe n'emirembe.

 Laba-eggye ly'abatukuvu 
 Wamu n'abo tusanyuke;
 Era bwe mba nga nzirise 
 Linzizaamu nnyo amaanyi

 Tetubalaba kaakano 
 Baamala okuwunguka;
 Naffe tuli mu lugendo 
 Tulibeera wamu nabo 
 
 Fenna awamu tuli mu ggwe,
 Tetubalaba n'amaaso,
 Naye olw'okukkiriza 
 Tuli mu kisibo kimu.
 
 Mu maaso go ffe awamu,
 Bulijjo tutendereza;
 Mu mwoyo tulabagana,
 Ne twebaza ekisa kyo.

 Eggye lyonna-ery'omu ggulu 
 Abo be ndibeera nabo;
 Era naawe Omwagalwa,
 Ani alitwawukanya?
