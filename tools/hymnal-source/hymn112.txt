AYI Kitaffe atwagala,
Tuwaayo gy'oli-omwana-ono 
Ayingire mu kisibo 
Anywezebwe mu kwagala.

Omwana wo omwagalwa 
Teyagoba baaba bato; 
Ne kaakano tumuwaayo 
Omunyweze mu mikono gyo.

Mu biro eby'okukemebwa 
Muwenga obuwanguzi, 
Ayolese-ekitiibwa kyo;
Bonna bakutendereze.

Era ne ku nkomerero 
Bw'olijja mu kitiibwa kyo,
Asanyuke okuweebwa 
Engule gye wasuubiza
